step 2 Calibrate your home row

  1. Rest your left fingers on A S D F and your right fingers on J K L ;. Feel for the bumps on F and J.
  2. Press Space with your thumb (or the Start button).
  3. Hold still for 3 seconds.

Ready when you are.

Map the keyboard

Now show the camera where the keys are. Press each key below once with the finger named. Your other fingers can leave the home row.

The app asks for one key at a time and names the finger to use. It records the camera and writes down which finger it asked for, so the recording comes out already labeled.

  • Press exactly the key shown, with exactly the finger shown, then go back to the home row.
  • About 1 in 5 prompts asks for a wrong finger on purpose. Do what it says: those are the mistakes your finger check must catch.
  • Pressed with the wrong finger by accident? Press Backspace and the last key is asked again.
  • About 100 prompts, roughly 5 minutes. Keep the camera still.

Move the file into the recordings folder of the project, then run npm run evaluate -- recordings. The labels are inside the file, so there is nothing else to write.
